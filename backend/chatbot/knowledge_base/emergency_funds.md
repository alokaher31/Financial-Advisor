# Emergency Fund Guidelines & Sizing

## What is an Emergency Fund?
An emergency fund is a liquid cash reserve kept exclusively for unforeseen essential expenses, such as unexpected medical emergencies, urgent auto/home repairs, or sudden job loss.

---

## Target Sizing Rules:
- **Single Income / Freelancer / Commission-Based**: Target **6 to 9 months** of essential living expenses (Needs).
- **Dual Income / Stable Salaried Household**: Target **3 to 6 months** of essential living expenses.
- **Starter Emergency Fund (Tier 1)**: For individuals actively carrying high-interest consumer debt, maintain a starter buffer of **$1,000 to $2,000 (or 1 month expenses)** before aggressively paying down debt.

---

## Recommended Account Types:
1. **High-Yield Savings Account (HYSA)**: FDIC/NCUA-insured accounts yielding competitive interest rates (e.g. 4.0% - 5.0%+ APY), providing high liquidity without market risk.
2. **Money Market Accounts (MMA)**: Bank-insured liquid accounts with check-writing or debit card capabilities.
3. **Important Note**: Do NOT store emergency funds in volatile stocks, cryptocurrency, or illiquid retirement accounts with withdrawal penalties.
