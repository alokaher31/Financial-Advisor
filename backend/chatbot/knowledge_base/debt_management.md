# Debt Management & Repayment Strategies

## 1. High-Interest Debt vs. Low-Interest Debt
- **High-Interest Debt (Toxic Debt)**: Any debt with an interest rate above **7% - 8% APR** (Credit cards, payday loans, high-rate personal loans). This should be aggressively repaid before investing beyond 401(k) employer matches.
- **Low-Interest Debt (Manageable Debt)**: Mortgages or low-rate student loans (< 4% - 5% APR). Standard scheduled payments are usually optimal while directing excess cash toward retirement or index investments.

---

## 2. Debt Payoff Strategies:

### A. The Debt Avalanche Method (Mathematically Optimal)
1. List all debts ordered by **highest interest rate (APR)** to lowest.
2. Pay minimums on all accounts.
3. Funnel all extra funds to the balance with the highest APR.
4. **Benefit**: Minimizes total interest paid over time and accelerates debt freedom mathematically.

### B. The Debt Snowball Method (Psychologically Motivating)
1. List all debts ordered by **smallest balance** to largest, regardless of interest rate.
2. Pay minimums on all accounts.
3. Funnel extra cash toward eliminating the smallest balance first.
4. **Benefit**: Provides rapid psychological wins and momentum to stay motivated.

---

## 3. Debt Consolidation & Refinancing
- **Balance Transfer Cards**: 0% introductory APR cards (typically 12-21 months) can pause interest accumulation during aggressive debt payoff. Watch for 3%-5% transfer fees.
- **Personal Consolidation Loans**: Replaces multiple high-rate credit card balances with a single fixed-rate loan at a lower APR.
