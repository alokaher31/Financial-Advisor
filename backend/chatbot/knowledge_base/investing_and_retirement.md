# Investing Principles & Retirement Planning

## 1. The Financial Priority Order (Waterfall Method)
Follow this prioritized order for allocating savings:
1. **Employer 401(k) Match**: Always contribute enough to capture 100% of employer matching (e.g. 50% match up to 6% salary = immediate 50% return).
2. **Eliminate High-Interest Debt**: Aggressively clear debts with APR > 7%.
3. **Emergency Fund**: Complete 3–6 months of essential living expenses in an HYSA.
4. **Health Savings Account (HSA)**: If enrolled in a high-deductible health plan (triple tax-advantaged).
5. **Roth IRA / Traditional IRA**: Maximize annual individual retirement contributions.
6. **Max 401(k) / 403(b)**: Up to annual IRS contribution limits.
7. **Taxable Brokerage Accounts**: Broad-market low-cost index funds / ETFs.

---

## 2. Asset Allocation by Risk Profile
- **Aggressive (High Risk / 15+ Year Horizon)**: 85%–90% Equities (e.g. Total US Stock Market + International Index Funds), 10%–15% Fixed Income/Bonds.
- **Moderate (Medium Risk / 7-15 Year Horizon)**: 70% Equities, 30% Bonds and Real Estate / Dividend assets.
- **Conservative (Low Risk / < 5 Year Horizon or Near Retirement)**: 40%–50% Equities, 50%–60% Short-term bonds, Treasury bills, and Cash equivalents.

---

## 3. Core Investment Vehicles:
- **Broad Market Index Funds / ETFs**: S&P 500 (VOO/SPY), Total Stock Market (VTI), Total International (VXUS).
- **Dollar-Cost Averaging (DCA)**: Investing a fixed sum on a regular schedule (e.g. bi-weekly) regardless of market fluctuations to reduce timing risk.
- **Compound Interest**: Returns generate their own returns exponentially over decades: `A = P(1 + r/n)^(nt)`.
