# Budgeting Strategies & Methodologies

## 1. The 50/30/20 Budgeting Framework
The 50/30/20 rule is a foundational guideline dividing after-tax monthly income into three distinct categories:
- **50% for Needs (Essential Expenses)**: Housing (rent/mortgage), utilities, groceries, basic transportation, essential insurance, and minimum required debt payments.
- **30% for Wants (Lifestyle Choices)**: Dining out, entertainment, shopping, vacations, subscription services, and non-essential hobbies.
- **20% for Savings & Debt Paydown**: High-yield emergency fund contributions, 401(k)/IRA investments, and debt payments above the minimum.

### How to Apply:
1. Calculate Net (Take-Home) Income per month.
2. Cap mandatory fixed living costs at 50%.
3. If fixed expenses exceed 50%, reduce 'Wants' until fixed costs are stabilized.

---

## 2. Zero-Based Budgeting (ZBB)
In Zero-Based Budgeting, every dollar of monthly income is assigned a specific purpose before the month begins:
`Income - (Expenses + Savings + Debt Paydown) = $0`
- **Ideal for**: Individuals who want maximum financial discipline and precision.
- **Implementation**: Allocate fixed costs first, then automated savings/investments, then variable spending envelopes.

---

## 3. The Envelope Budgeting System
A cash or digital sub-account budgeting method where funds for variable categories (e.g. groceries, entertainment, dining) are segregated into separate 'envelopes'. Once an envelope is empty, spending in that category halts until the next month.
